Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 10 September 2026

These are the standards that Dhodia Lagna Parichay ("the App") follows to prevent, detect, and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and any other content or conduct that endangers a minor. The App is operated by Let's Solve Together, Gujarat, India.

1. The App is for adults only

Dhodia Lagna Parichay is a matrimony service for adults of legal marriage age in India — 21 for men and 18 for women. Anyone below these ages is prohibited from creating an account or using the App, as stated in our Terms & Conditions.

  • Every member signs in with a Google account and provides a date of birth during onboarding; profiles that do not meet the age requirement are not permitted.
  • The App is rated for a mature audience on the app stores and is not directed at, marketed to, or designed for children.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and for any content, messages, contact details, or behaviour that sexualises, exploits, or endangers a minor. Accounts involved in such activity are removed immediately and permanently.

3. How to report

If you encounter a profile, photo, or any content on the App that involves or endangers a child, report it right away:

  • In the App: open the member's profile, use Report, and select the reason. Reports are reviewed by our team.
  • By email: write to shreyaspatel29@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report" and any details or screenshots.

4. How we respond

  • Reports of CSAE are prioritised and actioned as quickly as possible.
  • Confirmed CSAE content is removed and the associated account is banned.
  • We preserve relevant records and report apparent CSAM to the appropriate authorities, including India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and, where applicable, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), in line with applicable law.
  • Members can also block any other member at any time.

We acknowledge child safety reports within 24 hours and act on confirmed cases immediately.

6. Compliance

These standards are maintained to comply with the Google Play Child Safety Standards Policy and applicable Indian law, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.
