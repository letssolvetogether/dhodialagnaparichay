Deleting Your Account and Data

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Dhodia Lagna Parichay account and the personal data associated with it, and how to remove individual pieces of data without deleting your account. The App is operated by Let's Solve Together, Gujarat, India.

Deleting specific data (keeping your account)

You can remove individual data yourself from inside the app, without deleting your account:

  • Photos — on the Profile / Edit screen, tap a photo and choose remove. The photo and its stored file are deleted immediately.
  • Phone number and Instagram handle — open the Contact screen, clear the field, and save. The value is erased from our servers.
  • Other profile fields can be changed at any time from the Edit screen.

To delete your entire account and all data, use one of the two options below.

Option 1 — Delete your account from inside the app

This is the fastest way and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. On the Home screen, tap the menu icon (☰) in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Delete Account and confirm.
  4. Your profile, photos, interests and requests are erased straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Request account deletion by email

Use this if you have removed the app or cannot sign in.

Email shreyaspatel29@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign in, with the subject "Account deletion request".

We will acknowledge your request within 24 hours and complete the deletion within 15 days, after confirming the request came from the account owner.

What is deleted

The following are erased permanently and cannot be recovered:

  • Your profile and all profile details (name, gender, date of birth, height, marital status, native place, work and family location, caste, kul, working status, LinkedIn handle)
  • Your contact details (Instagram handle, phone number)
  • All photographs you uploaded, including the stored image files
  • Interest requests you have sent or received
  • Records of profiles you have viewed
  • Your sign-in sessions
  • Your user account itself, including the link to your Google account

What is kept, and why

When an account is deleted we retain one small deletion record containing your email address, an internal user identifier and the date of deletion. We keep this to prevent abuse — for example, someone repeatedly deleting and recreating an account to evade a suspension or a report — and to keep a record that the deletion took place.

If you were ever reported, suspended or the subject of a moderation action, that moderation history is also kept. It does not contain your profile content.

Nothing else is retained. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how data is handled.
